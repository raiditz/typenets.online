Letter unlocking

With unlocking on, guided practice focuses on the letters you have unlocked so far. It uses your all-time key statistics and your target speed from Settings, and also needs at least 75% all-time accuracy. After a completed session, one new letter may unlock when your current set meets the requirements.

Turning unlocking off opens the full alphabet — it never erases your saved letter history.

05 Questions
Where is my progress saved?

Practice history and preferences are saved in this browser. Clearing site data can erase local-only history, so keep using the same browser to keep your scores and statistics together.

Do I need an account?

No. You can practise, test and race without one. Multiplayer and the leaderboard use Firebase anonymous sign-in behind the scenes; signing in simply lets your profile follow you.

How does the high-score list work?

The Typing test page ranks the all-time best performers. Finish a solo test with solid accuracy and your best result can earn a place under your chosen leaderboard name.

Something looks wrong — what should I try?

Refresh the page once and check your internet connection. Multiplayer and the leaderboard both need a live connection.
