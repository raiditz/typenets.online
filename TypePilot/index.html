About TypePilot

TypePilot is a free typing tutor that helps you learn touch typing and type faster with fewer mistakes. Typing practice adapts to your weakest letters, 34 guided lessons teach finger placement with drills and games, the typing test measures your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy, and multiplayer races let you compete with up to five players. Everything runs in your browser, with no download and no sign-up required.
